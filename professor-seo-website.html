Research Narrative

About Dr. Seo

Dr. Youngguk Seo studies the behavior of multi-phased transportation materials, including asphalt and cement concrete mixtures, which exhibit various mechanical responses under mechanical and environmental loads. His research focuses on fracture and fatigue failures, developing predictive models to enhance pavement design guides and management systems.

Additionally, his work extends to sustainable infrastructure, including greenhouse gas emission quantification during highway construction and operation, and exploring renewable energy solutions for snow melting.

Since 2004, Dr. Seo has expanded his research to full-scale field experiments, leading multiple national projects at the Korea Expressway Corporation Test Road (KECTR), a 7.7 km highway test facility in South Korea. These studies have contributed to the calibration and validation of mechanistic-empirical pavement design methods.

From 2013 to 2014, as a pavement engineer at the Turner-Fairbank Highway Research Center (TFHRC) of the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), Dr. Seo conducted a performance ranking study on recycled materials, including Recycled Asphalt Pavement (RAP) and Recycled Asphalt Shingles (RAS), using Accelerated Loading Facilities (ALF).

Areas of Focus

Research Interests

01
Full-Scale Performance Evaluation
Field experiments at the Korea Expressway Corporation Test Road (KECTR) and TFHRC, contributing to mechanistic-empirical pavement design calibration.
02
Laboratory Material Characterization
Multi-phased asphalt and cement concrete mixtures under mechanical and environmental loads, including fracture process zone and fatigue damage analysis.
03
Sustainable Infrastructure & Renewable Energy
Geothermal snow melting systems, ground-source heat pumps, GHG emission modeling from road construction and traffic, cool pavement, and recycled materials.
04
AI & Deep Learning in Pavement
Structural and functional performance predictions of road structures using DL/ML models; tire-pavement noise characterization via machine learning.
