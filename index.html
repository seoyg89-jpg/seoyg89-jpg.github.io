About Dr. Seo

Dr. Youngguk Seo is a Professor in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Kennesaw State University (KSU), where he has been a faculty member since 2014. He earned his Ph.D. in Transportation Materials from North Carolina State University (2003), his M.S. from Seoul National University, and his B.E. from the University of Seoul — graduating first in his class (Summa Cum Laude).

Dr. Seo studies the behavior of multi-phased transportation materials, including asphalt and cement concrete mixtures, under mechanical and environmental loads. His research focuses on fracture and fatigue failures, developing predictive models to enhance pavement design guides and management systems.

His work also extends to sustainable infrastructure — including greenhouse gas emission quantification during highway construction and operation, renewable energy solutions for snow melting, and industrial waste incorporation in construction materials. Most recently, his lab applies deep learning and machine learning models to predict road structural and functional performance.

Since 2004, Dr. Seo has led multiple national projects at the Korea Expressway Corporation Test Road (KECTR), a 7.7 km highway test facility in South Korea, contributing to the calibration and validation of mechanistic-empirical pavement design methods. From 2013 to 2014, as a pavement engineer at the Turner-Fairbank Highway Research Center (TFHRC) of the FHWA, he conducted performance ranking studies on Recycled Asphalt Pavement (RAP) and Recycled Asphalt Shingles (RAS) using Accelerated Loading Facilities (ALF).

Areas of Focus

Research Interests

01
Full-Scale Performance Evaluation
Field experiments at the Korea Expressway Corporation Test Road (KECTR, 7.7 km) and TFHRC, contributing to calibration of mechanistic-empirical pavement design methods and real-world performance data.
02
Laboratory Material Characterization
Multi-phased asphalt and cement concrete mixtures under mechanical and environmental loads — including fracture process zone analysis, VECD modeling, digital image correlation, and acoustic emission monitoring.
03
Sustainable Infrastructure & Renewable Energy
Geothermal snow melting systems, ground-source heat pumps, GHG emission quantification from road construction and traffic, cool pavement, recycled materials (RAP/RAS), and industrial waste in concrete.
04
AI & Deep Learning in Pavement Engineering
Structural and functional performance predictions using DL/ML models; tire-pavement noise characterization via machine learning; hybrid forecasting of university energy demand; 3D concrete printing.
